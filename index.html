01 / UNIVERSITY OF CHICAGO · March – August 2026

In disordered neural networks, finite is different

Hopfield networks are models of associative memory: they can hold a stored pattern fixed or, with delayed non-reciprocal interactions, replay patterns as a sequence. I study how the transition between these regimes changes from the thermodynamic limit to finite disordered networks, where memories destabilise one by one.
