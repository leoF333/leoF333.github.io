From complex analysis to tropical geometry

Complex amoebas and their tropical limit

École Polytechnique
September 2024 – January 2025Supervisor
Omid AminiCollective mathematical essay
Collective essay · PDF · French ↗

The bridge

Complex amoebas retain the analytic geometry of polynomial zero sets. Tropicalisation exposes a piecewise-linear skeleton governed by Newton polygons.

The proof

The essay develops Ronkin functions, convexity and Hausdorff convergence, then uses Puiseux valuations to turn the limiting picture into an exact algebraic–tropical correspondence.

The scope

A collective reconstruction of standard results, not a claim of new theorems—and a sustained exercise in moving between algebraic, analytic and geometric representations.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

What survives when an algebraic variety is viewed only through asymptotic scale?

Methods

  • Proof-based mathematical exposition
  • Ronkin functions and convexity
  • Hausdorff convergence
  • Non-Archimedean valuations
