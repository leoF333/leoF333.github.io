RESEARCH / 02

Autoencoders and the statistical physics of high-dimensional learning

Data geometry, representation learning and generalisation

ENS Paris
September 2025 – March 2026Supervisor
Rémi MonassonResearch report
Research report · PDF · French ↗

The experiment

A single angular velocity generates 7,840-dimensional videos. By changing only the rotating shape, the data moves from spectrally simple to high-frequency and discontinuous.

The result

A shallow encoder and deep decoder recover smooth latent dynamics across an unseen interval. Sharp edges produce heavy covariance tails and a sharp failure of interpolation.

The theory

Applying advanced statistical physics techniques such as the replica method to the neural network rigorously links data covariance spectra and the sample-to-dimension ratio to the autoencoder’s training error.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Which statistical properties of high-dimensional data make its latent dynamics learnable?

Methods

  • Synthetic data and controlled interpolation experiments
  • Neural-network training
  • Covariance eigenvalue analysis
  • Replica-symmetric calculation
